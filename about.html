About GlobalPath International

We connect skilled workers with verified employers abroad — transparently, ethically, and without hidden fees.

Who We Are

GlobalPath International is a job placement service that helps skilled workers find legitimate employment opportunities in the United Kingdom, Europe, and North America.

We were founded to solve a specific problem: too many workers pay agents who disappear, or accept jobs that don't exist. We exist to make overseas work safe, verified, and transparent.

Every employer we work with is checked. Every job we list is real. Every fee we charge is shown upfront.

Our Mission

To make international job placement accessible, honest, and safe for every worker who wants to build a better life abroad.

We believe that the opportunity to work abroad should not depend on knowing the right person, or on risking your savings with an agent who might disappear. It should depend on your skills and your willingness to work.

What We Stand For

✅ Verified Employers Only

We verify every employer before listing their role. No fake jobs. No ghost listings.

💰 No Hidden Fees

You see the exact cost before you commit. No surprise charges after you apply.

🤝 Real Human Support

Our agents are reachable on Telegram and respond to every applicant personally.

📄 Transparent Process

You know where your application stands at every stage. No black boxes.

How We Work

  1. You apply through our website or Telegram bot.
  2. We match your profile to verified employers hiring in your field.
  3. You review the matching sponsors and decide whether to proceed.
  4. We prepare your documents and coordinate with the employer.
  5. You get placed — with support until you start work.

Our Commitment to Ethical Placement

We do not charge workers for job offers. We do not misrepresent salaries, working conditions, or visa outcomes. We do not work with employers who have a record of exploiting migrant workers.

If you ever feel pressured, misled, or asked for money outside of our published fees, report it immediately to report@globalpathjobplacement.online.
