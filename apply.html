Before you continue — how work visas work

Please read this carefully.

⚠️ The truth about work visas

You cannot apply for a work visa without a job offer. Most countries require an employer to sponsor you before you can even apply.

Here's how it actually works:

  • First, you find and secure a job with an employer abroad
  • The employer sponsors your visa application
  • Then you submit your visa application with the job offer letter
  • The embassy reviews it and makes a decision

No job offer = no work visa.
