1. Who We Are

GlobalPath International ("we", "us", "our") operates globalpathjobplacement.online and provides international job placement services. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

If you have any questions, contact us at hello@globalpathjobplacement.online.

2. What Data We Collect

When you use our services, we may collect:

  • Identity data: full name, date of birth, nationality
  • Contact data: email address, phone number, Telegram username
  • Application data: job preferences, work experience, qualifications, passport status
  • Documents you upload: CV, passport scans, certificates (only when you send them)
  • Payment data: transaction reference (we do not store card details — payments are processed by Paystack)
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, pages visited (via analytics)
  • Communication data: messages you send us by email or Telegram

3. Why We Collect It

We use your data to:

  • Match you with verified employers hiring in your field
  • Process your application and communicate with you about its status
  • Share your profile with employers who have expressed interest (only with your consent)
  • Verify payments and prevent fraud
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

Legal basis: We process your data based on your consent (when you apply), the performance of a contract (our service to you), and our legitimate interest in operating a safe placement service.

4. Who We Share It With

We do not sell your data. We may share it with:

  • Employers and sponsors — only when you have chosen to proceed with a specific opportunity
  • Service providers — Supabase (database), Paystack (payments), Resend (email), Cloudflare (hosting)
  • Legal authorities — if required by law

5. How Long We Keep It

We retain your application data for as long as your account is active, or for up to 24 months after your last activity. Documents you upload are kept for 12 months after your placement or last contact, whichever is later.

You can request deletion at any time (see Section 7).

6. How We Protect It

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and secure third-party providers. However, no online system is 100% secure — we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications
  • Object to certain processing
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured format

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@globalpathjobplacement.online. We respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies & Analytics

We use minimal cookies and may use analytics tools (such as Cloudflare Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site. These do not identify you personally.

If you reach our site through a LinkedIn ad, the LinkedIn Insight Tag may be used to measure ad performance and show relevant ads. You can opt out of LinkedIn's tracking through your LinkedIn ad settings.

9. Children

Our services are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when. Continued use of our services means you accept the updated policy.
