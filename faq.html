Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about applying, fees, and timelines.

🚀 Getting Started

How do I apply for a job abroad?
Click "Apply" in the menu, fill in your details, and submit. It takes about 2 minutes. You'll get a reference number (like GP-2026-12345) to track your application.
Is it free to apply?
Yes — browsing jobs and submitting your application is completely free. You only pay when you choose to unlock sponsor contact details for a specific opportunity.
What countries do you place workers in?
We currently focus on the United Kingdom, with expanding opportunities in Europe (Germany, Netherlands) and North America (Canada). Available countries change based on employer demand.
Do I need experience to apply?
It depends on the role. Many warehouse, care, and hospitality jobs accept entry-level applicants. Skilled roles (HGV, nursing, engineering) require relevant qualifications. Each job listing states its requirements.

💰 Fees & Payment

How much does it cost to unlock sponsors?
Sponsor unlock is $9 USD. This gives you access to verified sponsor contact details matched to your profile.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. The only fee we charge directly is the sponsor unlock fee. Any employer-side costs (visa fees, medical, etc.) are separate and will be clearly explained by the employer before you commit.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major debit and credit cards, and other secure online payment methods. We do not accept cryptocurrency, gift cards, or payments to personal accounts.
What if I pay and don't get placed?
The unlock fee gives you access to sponsor contacts — it does not guarantee placement, because employers make final hiring decisions. If you unlock sponsors and none match your profile, contact us and we'll either rematch you or refund the fee.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes. If no sponsor matches your profile after unlocking, you can request a full refund within 7 days. Email hello@globalpathjobplacement.online with your reference number.

📋 Application Process

How long does the process take?
From application to placement typically takes 4–12 weeks, depending on the role, country, and how quickly your documents are ready. Visa processing alone can take 4–8 weeks.
How do I track my application?
Use your reference number on our tracking page, or message our Telegram bot with your reference and it will show your status instantly.
What documents do I need?
At minimum: a valid passport, CV, and any relevant certificates. Some roles require police clearance, medical certificates, or proof of qualifications. We'll tell you exactly what's needed once you're matched.
Can I apply for multiple jobs?
Yes — but we recommend focusing on one application at a time so we can give you proper support. If you want to apply for a different role, contact us and we'll update your file.

🛡️ Trust & Safety

How do I know the jobs are real?
Every employer we list is verified before their role goes live. If a job turns out to be fake, we remove it immediately and investigate. Report suspicious listings to hello@globalpathjobplacement.online.
How can I tell if an agent is really from GlobalPath?
All our official communication comes from @globalpath_support_bot on Telegram, or from an @globalpathjobplacement.online email. We will never ask you to pay into a personal account or via gift cards/crypto.
What should I do if someone asks me for money pretending to be GlobalPath?
Report it immediately to hello@globalpathjobplacement.online. Do not send money. We will never ask for payment outside our published sponsor unlock fee.
Do you guarantee a visa?
No — and be suspicious of anyone who does. Visas are granted by government authorities, not by us or by employers. We help you prepare a strong application and connect you with employers who sponsor, but the final decision is always the government's.
