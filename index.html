Visa Categories

Understand the main visa types before you apply.

🧳

Tourist Visa

For short visits, holidays, and family trips. Usually valid for 30–90 days.

💼

Work Visa

For employment abroad. Requires a job offer and employer sponsorship in most cases.

🎓

Student Visa

For study programs. Requires admission letter and proof of funds.

🚏

Transit Visa

For passing through a country en route to another destination.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Visa

For joining a spouse, parent, or child who is a resident or citizen.

🏥

Medical Visa

For medical treatment abroad. Requires hospital invitation and records.
